The [Entelect](https://entelect.com.au/) model is to assemble teams of developers, business analysts, and designers and
deploy them onsite for client projects. These engagements can be short term or effectively permanent. Team members can
rotate amongst projects and clients. To support this model and boost the chances of success, Entelect focuses on the
growth of its staff.

As a junior developer, this was the perfect place to start. We were encouraged to participate in trainings, hackathons,
conferences, developer events, and more, some organized by Entelect, others external.

After spending a few weeks onboarding and joining the aforementioned learning opportunities, I joined a team working on
a greenfield Spring Boot application. My placement was so brief that I cannot recall the client, the domain, or what we
were building.

I was then asked to join an established team working at an investment back office that processed investment transactions
for a large investment bank, Investec (now Ninety One??).
